# Shadcn workspace example

## Included flow

People can inspect a sample dashboard, search fictional records, open record details and save example preferences in their own browser. Anyone with the preview can use it without an account. There is no backend, authentication, team access model or email delivery.

Records reset with the app's fixtures; settings remain in browser storage until cleared. Changing a form field clears prior save confirmation. Storage failures explain why settings were not saved.

## Boundaries

These are accepted behaviors of the starter example, not requirements for a consuming product. Keep new product assumptions explicit. Offer guidance setup when a new flow needs missing approved context; do not treat setup drafts as active policy.
